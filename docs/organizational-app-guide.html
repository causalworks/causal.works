The Cooperative App, Explained

A plain-language tour of Cooperative: what each area is for and how they connect.

Cooperative is the sector-neutral operational core of Causal's platform, for any nonprofit or coalition forming around collaborative work and common needs: climate action, civic action, social work, cultural work, and more. Each organization gets a workspace with budgeting, accounting, funding, contacts, compliance, and reporting, plus membership and fiscal-sponsorship tools where used. The tools are built around a common needs framework, and every organization's instance is completely private to it. Collaboration happens separately, in Workshop and the shared cooperative resources: the peer network, work library, and work pool.

AreasThe workspace, area by area

Workspace Picker

The landing page after login: a greeting, then your organizations and pending invites in one list, each with your role, an attention status, and when you last visited. Accept an invite here, create a workspace, or open "Manage organizations". A Sandbox section offers Demo Company, a shared practice workspace. Joining an existing organization is invite-only.

Dashboard

The landing page inside an organization: a "Since your last visit" summary, a Cooperative panel, and a "Needs attention" feed (unreviewed imports, upcoming renewals, expiring documents, outstanding receipt follow-ups).

Budget

The financial planning core, and home of the chart of accounts and programs. A twelve-month grid compares planned budget to actual spending by program and grant. Tabs:

  • Personnel: salaries and benefits, year-over-year copy, and a CSV template import that can split a person across programs
  • Insurance / Other Schedules: recurring costs spread across the year
  • Indirect Costs: costs calculated from the rest of the budget
  • Scenarios: draft budgets branched from the live one and compared side by side, either as a quick overlay or as a detailed fork of the underlying inputs. Promoting one to live takes an admin or finance role.

Budget Settings holds the chart of accounts, programs and activities, CSV imports, and a recode tool. A new organization gets a setup wizard: start from a standard nonprofit template, pull accounts from Xero, or upload a CSV.

Accounting

Budget is the plan; Accounting is the record of what actually happened. Its eleven tabs cover the ledger, bank reconciliation, bills, invoices, fixed assets, expense claims, board designations, recoding, and gift postings. Detail is in the Accounting guide.

Causal's own ledger is opt-in per organization and coexists with Xero. Hard accounting rules (balanced entries, locked fiscal years, no cross-organization references) are enforced by the database. Anything recorded in Funding or Membership becomes a ledger entry only when someone reviews and posts it in Accounting, never automatically.

Funding

One page with two tabs.

  • Grants: every grant applied for or received, with funder, amounts, dates, status, and next report due. Clicking a row opens a panel, and uploading a grant agreement can prefill it for review. Grants can be allocated across programs and years, flagged as federal awards, given an indirect cost rate, and exported as PDF reports. Where a grant posts in the ledger is set from Accounting, not here.
  • Contributions: gifts and pledges, including in-kind gifts and multi-year pledges, with acknowledgments and donation receipts. A gift can be designated to a sponsored project.

Contacts

Every donor, vendor, and customer in one list shared across Accounting and Funding. A contact can be several things at once. Vendors carry tax ID and W-9 status, and an admin can export a calendar-year 1099 summary as CSV (tracking only, no e-filing).

Compliance

Two tabs. IRS Form 990 holds preparation views (Part IX functional expenses, Schedules B, D, G, I, J, and O) and the functional-expense classification. Obligations tracks other recurring filings and renewals, sorted into coming up and overdue.

Reports

The export hub: board report, 990 Part IX, balance sheet, budget views, giving summary, cash forecast, activity log, and the four standard nonprofit financial statements (Financial Position, Activities, Functional Expenses, Cash Flows), computed live from the ledger with net assets split into without and with donor restrictions.

Reconciliation & Close runs tie-out checks for a fiscal year and offers a reversible lock. An admin can freeze a year, then reopen it with a required reason. Locking is advisory, so an organization can lock an imperfect year on purpose while an audit is in progress.

Library

Shared cooperative resources: financial report, grant, and board-material templates. Also reachable as a Cooperative tab, so the same content has two entry points.

Document Vault

Two tabs. All documents: upload by category, set expiration dates and retention periods, attach expense-claim receipts, and share with one person or a standing group. Audit binder: a fiscal-year checklist of 17 expected document types against what has been uploaded. Documents here are what syncs to the organization's Solid pod.

Solid Pod

Data sovereignty: the organization's documents live in its own Solid pod, outside Causal's database. This page shows pod status, sync, the documents in the pod, Causal's own access (revocable here at any time), and who has been given access: individuals, standing groups, or one-time email-verified links for people without a Solid identity. Revoking a link stops access at once. It covers documents only. Full detail and current gaps are in the Platform guide.

Membership

For organizations with a paying membership program: Members, Tiers, Renewals, and Payments. A dues payment reaches the ledger through Bank Reconciliation when the deposit is coded, the same way cash gifts do.

Sponsorship

For organizations acting as fiscal sponsor; turned on under Settings. Each project is Standard (pass-through) or Managed (comprehensive). Standard projects track designated gifts, disbursements to the sponsee net of the admin fee (each needs a separate approval), the balance, and when the sponsee's next spending report is due. Managed projects link to their own activity under a program in the sponsor's regular books.

Cooperative

The one space shared across organizations, and where they collaborate. Workshop is the default tab.

  • Members: a directory of the organizations in the cooperative
  • Library: shared resources and templates
  • Work Pool: tasks any member organization can post or pick up
  • Workshop: collaborative projects with threads, messages, proposals, documents, and a Systems Map for loop and cascade mapping. It includes the Intervention Modeler, which suggests which of Donella Meadows' 12 leverage-point levels a proposed intervention works at. Still developing, and not yet reviewed by anyone with real systems-change practice. Workshop content is the one thing that can be visible across organizations.
  • Engagement: a thin, read-only list of items tagged as engagement artifacts from the shared library

Settings

An organization's own configuration: profile and fiscal year; workspace users, roles, and program assignments; integrations (Xero; QuickBooks is marked coming soon); the fiscal-sponsorship and membership switches; and Security, where an admin can lengthen the session timeout (15 minutes to 8 hours). Expense-claim rules are set on the Expense Claims tab in Accounting, and the chart of accounts lives in Budget Settings.

BackendBackend & integrations

Mostly deterministic accounting and CRM logic, plus a few Gemini-backed features that only pre-fill or suggest.

Access control
Every org-scoped request checks that you are logged in, that the account has Cooperative access, and that you belong to this organization, which also resolves your role. The database enforces the same boundaries with row-level security, plus a program-level policy for the Program role.
Gemini (gemini-2.5-flash)
The Workshop's Intervention Modeler prompts Gemini with Meadows' leverage-point hierarchy and the Earth4All turnarounds. Uploading a receipt, a scanned bill, invoice, or credit note, or a grant contract sends the file to Gemini to read out fields like vendor, date, amount, or grant terms. Bills and invoices are read for header fields only; account and program suggestions come from how that vendor was coded before. Reading only pre-fills the form, and nothing is saved until a person reviews it.
Xero
A per-organization OAuth2 connection with an encrypted refresh token. Once connected it reads the chart of accounts and tracking categories, maps them to Causal accounts and programs, and imports P&L actuals. The one write back to Xero is account-name updates. Disconnecting or deleting imported actuals is permanent.
Plaid
Bank Reconciliation can connect a bank through Plaid Link, syncing into the same queue CSV import fills. Manual sync is verified; the webhook signature check hasn't been exercised against a real delivery.

Rough edges and what's next

Rough today

  • Deleting all synced financial data (Settings → Integrations) is irreversible, with no confirmation step beyond the button.
  • Membership and Sponsorship appear in the sidebar only when turned on; a direct link still works when hidden.
  • Open financial statements are not filtered by program.
  • The Solid identity login is a prototype. See the Platform guide.

Next

  • Finished roles. Program scoping is enforced in the database, but per-action role checks for much of the app are still to do.
  • More data in the pod. Generated reports first, then periodic pod copies of live data such as budgets. Workshops would sync a one-way copy to each participating organization's pod.
  • QuickBooks integration. Shown as coming soon in Settings.
See the Platform guide for login, roles, and the Solid picture above this app, and the Accounting guide for that area in depth.

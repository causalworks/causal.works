Vendors and 1099

What is counted. Payments on bills to vendors marked 1099 eligible (Contacts, Edit compliance), on lines whose account, or the line itself, is marked reportable. Totals are by calendar year of payment.

What is left out. Payments by credit or debit card or through a payment network, which the processor reports on a 1099-K. Payments to corporations are generally exempt, except for legal and medical services; set the vendor's entity type in Edit compliance.

Filing. Forms are due to vendors and the IRS by January 31 for the year before. We track totals and export a CSV; we do not file with the IRS.

Each vendor's W-9, tax ID, entity type and 1099 eligibility are on the contact (Contacts, vendors, Edit compliance).

Invoice and bill numbers, payment terms, mileage rate

Each invoice takes the next number when it is sent, and each bill when it is approved. Drafts have no number until then, though you can type one. A number cannot be changed after it is sent or approved, and two documents cannot share one. Set the next number to carry on from numbers used in another system. Payment terms fill the due date of a new invoice or bill; each document can still change it. The mileage rate fills the amount on an expense claim line from its miles.

Conversion balances

For an organization moving its books here without importing the detail. Enter the balance of each account on the conversion date, the day before the first transaction recorded here, from the last closing trial balance. Unpaid invoices and bills are not entered here: record them as invoices and bills dated before the conversion date. Fixed asset cost is entered here too, and then added to the register with its depreciation to date. Once the balances are right, lock them. Changing them later needs an unlock with a reason.

Board-designated funds are internal earmarks of net assets without donor restriction (e.g. an operating reserve or building fund) — not a separate legal restriction, but a board policy the org tracks for its own liquidity disclosure. To move money into or out of a designation, post a manual transaction on the Transactions tab using your org's own net-assets-without-restriction account(s): tag only the line representing the designated side of the transfer with this designation, and leave the offsetting line untagged — the balance shown here is a running sum of the tagged side only, so tagging both lines of the same transfer would always net to zero.

Board-designated funds are internal earmarks of net assets without donor restriction (e.g. an operating reserve or building fund) — not a separate legal restriction, but a board policy the org tracks for its own liquidity disclosure. To move money into or out of a designation, post a manual transaction on the Transactions tab using your org's own net-assets-without-restriction account(s): tag only the line representing the designated side of the transfer with this designation, and leave the offsetting line untagged — the balance shown here is a running sum of the tagged side only, so tagging both lines of the same transfer would always net to zero.

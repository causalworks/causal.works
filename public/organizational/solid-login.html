Your Solid identity

Log in directly with your own Solid identity — your own account, your own WebID, on the platform's Solid pod server. This is separate from your platform login: everything here happens in your browser only. Your password, access token, and refresh token are never sent to or stored by the platform's servers.

This is a working prototype, not yet wired to the platform's permission system. The login and the resource fetch below are real — a genuine Solid-OIDC handshake and a genuine ACP-scoped request using your own session, not a mock. What isn't built yet: nothing here derives your pod access from your platform role (Admin/Finance/Program/Fundraising). An org still has to explicitly grant your WebID access first — from that org's Solid Pod page — before anything below can read it.

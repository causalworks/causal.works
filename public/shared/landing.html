agency — for individuals
A personal tracker for civic and financial action
Your elected officials, your bank, and the organizations you give to all take actions that affect you. Agency tracks what they're doing, and surfaces the petitions, events, and volunteer opportunities most relevant to you. A ledger keeps a record of what you've done, where your money sits, and what it's funding.
cooperative — for organizations
A shared workspace for coordination
Member directory, shared library, and Workshop — where orgs map interventions together using systems-thinking tools — give the network a place to coordinate. Your financial data stays yours: budget planning, grants and donor pipeline, Xero actuals, and 990 compliance run on your own private chart of accounts, separate from what you share with the network.

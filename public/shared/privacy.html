What we collect and why

Account

Your email address is used to send you a magic link to sign in. We do not store passwords. Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) — this is necessary to provide the service you've signed up for.

Location

A street address you provide is used to identify your elected representatives via the Google Civic Information API. A primary region you set is used to surface relevant organisations and actions. Neither is shared with third parties for any other purpose. Legal basis: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) — providing this is optional and you can remove it at any time in Settings.

Action data

When you mark an action as done or dismiss it, that engagement is stored to personalise your experience and maintain your contribution record. This data is associated with your account and is not shared. Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Bank check

When you check a bank, Causal receives only the bank name you type in. We do not receive your account number, balance, transaction history, or login credentials. Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) — you initiate this check.

Email processing

Causal subscribes to the newsletters and action alerts of the organisations listed on the platform, using addresses it manages. These emails are organisational communications, not personal mail. They are read by our AI pipeline to create the action cards shown in the app, and we keep the text of each email so that extraction can be checked and corrected.

If you choose to give an organisation your personal Causal address (for example, so that donation receipts and responses appear in your Ledger), the email is read when it arrives to extract action information, and we do not save its full text. We keep the sender, the subject and a short preview (roughly the first 200 characters) so that your Responses list can show it. Information extracted from such an email, such as the organisation, the request and any deadline, may appear as a card for other people who follow that organisation. Legal basis: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) — this is an opt-in feature.

Usage data

Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) are retained for up to 30 days for security and diagnostic purposes only. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — keeping the platform secure and diagnosing problems.

Organization financial and donor data (Cooperative)

If you use the Cooperative workspace on behalf of an organization, budget figures, chart of accounts, grant and donor records, and documents you upload are stored under that organization's account and are visible only to members of that organization. Financial data synced from a connected accounting system (see Xero, below) is stored the same way. This data is not shared with other organizations on the platform, and is not used to train models or shown to other users outside your organization. Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) between Causal and your organization.

Document storage

Documents you upload to an organization's workspace are stored on Causal's servers. As part of our data-sovereignty work described below, a limited pilot now also backs up some organizations' documents to a self-hosted Solid pod that Causal operates; this is a one-way backup, not a change in who can access your data today. Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • We do not share your data with third parties except as described in this policy
  • We do not build profiles for sale or targeting
  • We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes

Third-party services

Google Civic Information API — your street address is sent to Google to retrieve representative information. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Bank check data — Causal uses a lookup based on the bank name you provide to show a fossil-fuel flag (if any). No bank account connection is performed.

Mobilize — your zip code is sent to Mobilize to look up nearby civic events (protests, canvasses, trainings). Mobilize's privacy policy applies.

Postmark — transactional emails (magic links, forwarded confirmations, organization invites) are sent via Postmark. Email addresses are processed by Postmark for delivery only.

Xero — if your organization connects Xero, Causal reads your chart of accounts, tracking categories, and profit-and-loss data via OAuth to support budgeting and reporting. Causal writes account-name updates back to Xero and nothing more. This connection and the data it retrieves are scoped to your organization's workspace only.

ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer — when an organization is added to Causal, its name is looked up against ProPublica's public nonprofit database (EIN, city/state, NTEE code) to mark it as verified rather than self-reported.

Hetzner — your data is stored on servers operated by Hetzner Online GmbH in Helsinki, Finland (EU). Hetzner's data processing terms apply.

Mistral AI — email content submitted for action extraction is processed by Mistral AI's API (Mistral AI is a French company; its API processes data in the EU by default). We have switched off the setting that lets Mistral use API content to train its models. Mistral's API terms apply.

Gemini (Google) — documents uploaded to the Cooperative workspace for reading (receipts, bills, invoices and grant contracts), organisation summaries, and workshop suggestions are processed by Google's Gemini API. Google's API terms apply.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights: guy@causal.works. Deletion requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

Data retention

Account data is retained until you request deletion. Action engagement records are retained to maintain your contribution history. Server logs are retained for 30 days. Bank connection tokens are deleted when you disconnect your bank or delete your account.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Data at rest is stored on encrypted servers in the EU. Access to production data is restricted to authorised personnel only.

Our direction on data ownership

Causal's long-term position is that your civic and financial data belongs to you — not to the platforms that process it. We are building toward a model where users hold their own data and grant Causal read access rather than Causal holding your data on your behalf. This aligns with emerging open standards for user-controlled data storage, including the W3C Solid protocol and the principles of the EU's Data Governance Act.

We are early into this: a limited pilot now backs up some organizations' documents to a self-hosted Solid pod, one-way and Causal-operated, as a first step. This policy describes how we handle your data today. As our architecture evolves toward user data sovereignty, we will update this policy to reflect those changes. We will not make your data harder to move or delete as the platform develops — only easier.
